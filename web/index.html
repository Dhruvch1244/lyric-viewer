// What it does

Every song,
a live set.

It all runs in real time off the audio on your machine — the words, the colour, the motion.

01

Lyrics on the beat

Per-word timing and animation that land with the music instead of scrolling past it.

02

Live visualizer

Real MilkDrop presets and GPU swirl fields warping and pulsing with every drop.

03

Auto-transcribe

No synced lyrics? On-device Whisper writes the timings for you — fully offline.

04

Translate & romanize

Read along in your own script with AI or on-device translation and transliteration.

05

Reads your player

Picks up now-playing straight from Windows. No share-picker, no configuration.

06

Wallpaper mode

Send the whole thing behind your icons and run it as a living desktop.

// Free · get it your way

Ready when
the music is.

The Windows app is the full experience. macOS and Linux run the visuals and lyrics — reading your system's now-playing is Windows-only for now. The signed installer is about 6MB — no bundled browser runtime, unlike most desktop apps in this space.
